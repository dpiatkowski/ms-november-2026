# From Cloud to AI: Zurich’s Resilient Enterprise Blueprint

**Speaker(s):** Brad Foss, Gopal Kankati, Vikas Naru

## Summary

Zurich U.S. describes its journey from cloud to AI: a cloud migration and data center exit (the slides claim zero production incidents and exiting five months early), a modern workplace with devices managed in Intune and trusted identity, then Copilot for every employee launched enterprise-wide on April 1 with learning, champions and visible leadership. Two foundations are the platform (resilient cloud for a regulated business) and the workplace (ready devices, trusted identity, secure app access).

Adoption follows three pillars (personal productivity, individual and team agents, enterprise agents), with Copilot Studio agents connected to production platforms through governed MCP connectors, such as ZEUS, a single front door for IT and HR support. Results are customer-reported, and the slide deck carries a standard disclaimer that it is not insurance or legal advice.
