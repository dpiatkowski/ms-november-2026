# Agentify Your Business Processes for ERP Partner Opportunities​

**Speaker(s):** Dave Barlin, Jared Hall, Niels Jensen, Maitane Munoz, Gokul Ramesh, Greg Gant, Alan Kahn

## Summary

The session covers partner opportunities in agentic ERP with Dynamics 365. It sizes the digital transformation consulting market (analyst estimates around $204B in 2026, ranges vary) and argues the opportunity is reimagining business processes rather than installing software, contrasting today's "people as middleware" with a Frontier reality where people are agent bosses across Chat, Cowork, Code and Autopilot grounded in Microsoft IQ and Dynamics 365.

The slides note Microsoft as a Leader in three Gartner Magic Quadrants for cloud ERP (finance, service-centric and product-centric), and the session also covers the AI ERP vision and roadmap and FastTrack engineering support.
