# What Microsoft Benefit Is Right for Your Use Case?

**Speaker(s):** Kriti Jangid, Gaelle Thurau, Raid El Badarin-Deriy

## Summary

The session maps Microsoft's benefits programs to three personas: an employee launching a product (Copilot in apps, Microsoft IQ context, delegation to Cowork and agents), an operations leader (Dynamics 365 agents that spot supply issues, agents built with Copilot Studio), and a security lead (Agent 365, Entra, Purview and Defender to see, control and protect agents and data).

It introduces Frontier Accelerate, a simplified package of expertise (FastTrack, specialized partners, Cloud Accelerate Factory, AI readiness assessments), savings and investments (AI Flex Plan, Copilot trials, partner delivery funding) and skilling (in-a-day workshops, role-based learning). The slides note benefits are subject to eligibility, approval and availability, and a Telefónica Germany story illustrates a three-month structural rollout with Microsoft.
