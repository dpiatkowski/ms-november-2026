# Edge for Business: Browser security in the age of AI productivity

**Speaker(s):** Arunesh Chandra, Lindsay Kubasik

## Summary

The session presents Edge for Business as the enterprise browser for humans and agents, citing Omdia that most work happens in the browser. Agentic features include agentic browsing in Cowork (generally available, needs Copilot with usage-based billing), a managed agent browsing experience where agent sessions get their own identity and window (preview, E5), a skills recorder (preview, coming soon), token efficiency, remix, interactive briefs and writing assistance.

Safety is described as a system: a secure browser foundation that enforces DLP, enterprise data protection within tenant boundaries, policy-based AI features, agentic safeguards (browsing within the user's permissions with visual cues) and a protected session, integrated with Entra, Intune, Defender, Purview and Sentinel. A Forrester Total Economic Impact projection of up to $12.8M over three years is cited as a projected, not guaranteed, benefit.
