# Apps and agents: Business process transformation in Copilot Studio

**Speaker(s):** Ashvini Sharma, Clay Wesener

## Summary

The session positions Copilot Studio as the business-process layer between Microsoft Copilot (employee productivity) and Microsoft Foundry (developer platform), with Agent 365 for observability and control. Its pitch is one conversational experience that creates and refines apps, agents and workflows that work together, hosted and governed on a managed runtime (Copilot Managed Runtime).

The Microsoft Copilot harness provides an agentic loop for long-horizon tasks, accurate tool calling across connectors, Microsoft IQ, MCP and connected agents, native creation and editing of Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files, and a secure sandbox container. Slides show quality gains of +11.9 to +47.5 points and input tokens falling from 47.8K to 26.5K per conversation versus the standard Copilot Studio harness, all on one model. Three harnesses are described: Microsoft Copilot (complex long-running processes), customer conversation (voice and service) and standard (classic agents). Some capabilities are Frontier or preview.
