# Modernize with AI to expand your practice and unlock customer growth​

**Speaker(s):** Mayank Bhatia, Frederico Pravatta Rezende, Barry Wordell, Varun Dubey, Brendan Mislin

## Summary

The session helps partners modernize with AI to expand their practice. It argues agents make now the moment for modernization (AI readiness, security and cost), cites a Forrester TEI study (about 4x more partner services revenue per SMB customer when taken up the AI ramp, and about 10% higher gross margin with agentic delivery, per partner-reported data) and announces Azure Catalyst, with claims of 70% less time on migration and 50% less effort to upgrade apps.

TCS shares best practices from an agentic application modernization platform with pre-built archetypes (for example SOAP web services to REST APIs), ReadyWorks contributes, and a partner-of-the-year award and panel close the session. Figures are Microsoft- or study-reported.
