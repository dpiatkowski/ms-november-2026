# Turn your data into AI advantage with Training in Microsoft Foundry

**Speaker(s):** Vijay Aski, Meng Tang, Shengtao Ma, Simon Zhao

## Summary

The session covers specializing models on your own data in Foundry. It argues that a generic frontier model gives the same capability to competitors and value accrues to the vendor, while a loop of deploy, observe, evaluate, fine-tune and gate lets organizations keep their IP and lower cost. Slides show fine-tuned MAI models improving pass rates on tasks such as A/B-test page edits, bid analysis and 5G root-cause analysis against frontier baselines.

The recommended path starts with prompting, grounding and tools to prove value, then uses training to optimize: learn from expert examples (SFT), align with preference pairs (DPO), and practice multi-step tasks in reinforcement-learning environments with verifiable rewards. Foundry offerings include Managed Training (SFT/RFT/DPO, preview), Programmed Training on managed compute, and RL environments (private preview), plus new open backbones and MAI-Code models for fine-tuning and Multi-LoRA on managed compute (preview). Docusign and Heidi Health share customer experience.
