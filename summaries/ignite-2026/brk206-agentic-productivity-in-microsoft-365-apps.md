# Agentic productivity in Microsoft 365 apps

**Speaker(s):** Brian Jones, Derek Johnson

## Summary

The session moves Copilot in Office from chat (one prompt, one response) to outcomes, where Copilot plans and takes multi-step actions directly in the document, spreadsheet or presentation. Lessons cited: users start with the result they want, iteration is the real workflow, context drives trust, users want control (previews, citations, undo), and work spans apps.

Capabilities shown include Auto, which picks the best-fit model and effort per request; on-canvas Rewrite in Word and Formula AI in Excel (general availability); role-ready skills and connectors for legal (Harvey, iManage, Relativity, Legora), finance (FactSet, LSEG, Moody's, S&P Global) and sales (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Highspot); and recordable, shareable skills that capture a repeatable process.

Further items are Brand Kit (generally available in PowerPoint and Excel, Word coming soon), a change history that logs edits by people and agents side by side, and interactive content in PowerPoint, Excel and Word (Word in frontier preview).
