# Microsoft and SAP bring a connected system of intelligence

**Speaker(s):** Mike Hulme, Hiren Shah, Dan Garbett Garbett

## Summary

Microsoft and SAP describe a connected system of intelligence where Microsoft IQ grounds AI in business context. Azure for SAP highlights include certified compute (Mv4 VMs), CXL integration (preview) and an Azure Workload Center for SAP (preview), plus bringing SAP data into Copilot, Teams and Joule with Work IQ (coming soon), governing agents across Agent 365 and SAP Agent Hub, SAP TabPFN predictive models for tabular data in Foundry (now available), and unified SAP and non-SAP data in Fabric via BDC Connect (private preview, general availability March 2027) and Open Mirroring.

Coca-Cola describes its SAP S/4HANA-based digital transformation, consolidating more than 3,000 fragmented models into 54 AI-ready data products and reporting B2B portal order growth and improved NPS (customer-reported).
