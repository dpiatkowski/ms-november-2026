# Frontier Telecoms: Intelligence, Trust and the partners who build them

**Speaker(s):** Alessandra Antonelli, Cara Catalane, Manish Mangal

## Summary

The session covers Frontier telecoms: own the intelligence, rent the capability, and trust lets it act. It frames four pillars (AI-powered customer experiences, intelligent business operations, autonomous network, growth beyond connectivity) with a Telco IQ governed intelligence layer and trust plane.

A broadband-fault example contrasts four systems seeing four events with one shared context where agents detect, resolve impact, apply policy, notify customers, dispatch field work and write back (the slides cite that 38% of telecom churn is driven by network issues and that proactive intervention reduces churn 3-4x). Telco proof points are shown alongside Microsoft's own network, and partners are guided to become AI-first transformation partners (with up to $200 billion of new demand cited). Tech Mahindra shares a partner view.
