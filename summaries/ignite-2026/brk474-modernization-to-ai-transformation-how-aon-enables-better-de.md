# Modernization to AI transformation: How Aon enables better decisions

**Speaker(s):** Alex Knizhnik, Sudha Kumar, Sudipto Dasgupta, Magnus Roe

## Summary

Aon describes modernizing its technology to scale AI: rationalizing a fragmented estate of 2,200+ applications, moving 22 data centers and priority workloads to Azure and creating a trusted enterprise data layer before embedding AI. That foundation enabled governed LLM access through Colleague AI and a GenAI gateway in Microsoft Foundry as a single control plane across clouds, with Foundry patterns for document processing, summarization and data comparison across multiple models.

Aon's approach moves from individual productivity to business transformation: start with outcomes, redesign workflows, standardize patterns and scale (for example a certificate center supporting up to one million certificates annually, plus broker and claims copilots). It stresses being both AI-ready and human-ready, and governing agents with AI FinOps that measures cost per business outcome rather than tokens. Figures are customer-reported.
