# Beyond the pilot: How governments are scaling AI with confidence

**Speaker(s):** John Doyle, Mikko Laakso

## Summary

The session examines how governments scale AI with confidence beyond pilots, citing ThoughtLab and Capgemini data that ambition does not equal impact (few cities and government organizations have moved AI from pilot to full-scale production). It argues pilots prove technology, not readiness, and that an AI use case is a repeatable pattern with a clear challenge, defined workflow, workforce and agents, and measurable outcomes.

A growing library of use cases is organized across government, transportation and urban infrastructure, public safety and justice, public finance, and social services and public health, with the example "elevate agency decision-making with unified intelligence" (cross-agency data integration, policy impact analysis, decision records). The Finnish Tax Administration (Vero) shares its experience.
