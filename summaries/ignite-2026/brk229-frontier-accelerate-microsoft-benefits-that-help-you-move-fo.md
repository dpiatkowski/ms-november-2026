# Frontier Accelerate: Microsoft benefits that help you move forward

**Speaker(s):** Cyril Belikoff, Gaelle Thurau, Raid El Badarin-Deriy

## Summary

The session presents Frontier Accelerate, Microsoft's simplified benefit package built around three themes: trusted expertise (assessments, FastTrack, Cloud Accelerate Factory, specialized partners), savings and investments (pricing offers, project funding, flexible pricing and savings plans) and skilled teams (personalized learning and on-demand labs). Benefits are subject to eligibility, approval and availability.

Two announcements stand out. Azure Catalyst is described as one agentic experience across Azure Migrate, Azure Copilot and GitHub Copilot that discovers the estate, builds the business case, designs the landing zone and migrates or modernizes, with the slides claiming up to 70% faster modernization (Microsoft product data; results vary). The AI Flex Plan offers 1- or 3-year terms with monthly or upfront payment, decrements the Azure consumption commitment, and covers Copilot, GitHub AI, Foundry, Microsoft IQ, Perception and Security Copilot, available in MCA-E. A Telefónica Germany story is featured.
