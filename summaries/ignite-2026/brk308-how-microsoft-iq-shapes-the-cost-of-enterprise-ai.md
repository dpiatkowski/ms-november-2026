# How Microsoft IQ shapes the cost of enterprise AI

**Speaker(s):** Sonia Cuff

## Summary

The session frames the economics of enterprise AI: the model is one line item, while context inflation, bespoke retrieval per agent, iteration, refreshing knowledge, cost attribution and risk drive real cost. The argument is to build context once with Microsoft IQ and reuse it across agents, shifting the question from time saved per person to how fast the enterprise turns intelligence into outcomes.

A fictional pharmacy (Caldova Pharmacy) cold-chain scenario shows a retail agent on four IQs: Web IQ and Fabric IQ trigger on a fridge excursion, Fabric IQ assesses impact, Foundry IQ supplies cited policy and root cause, Work IQ finds on-call people, and Fabric IQ and Work IQ confirm. It was prototyped on Foundry Agent Service with illustrative figures, and the session covers the business case and ROI.
