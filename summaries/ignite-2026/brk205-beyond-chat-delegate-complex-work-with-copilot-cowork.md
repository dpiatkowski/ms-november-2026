# Beyond Chat: Delegate Complex Work with Copilot Cowork

**Speaker(s):** Pratyusha Avadhanula, Chris Whitten, Dave Chapman

## Summary

Copilot Cowork is framed as the shift from driving every step in chat to delegating an outcome: Cowork plans and runs the steps while the user reviews and approves key actions. It works on files in a folder or site, in Outlook, Teams, calendar and SharePoint, in the browser for systems without connectors, and with tools that take real actions such as sending, scheduling and updating.

A worked example is a scheduled Friday-noon prompt that reads the week's external meetings, pulls email, Teams and CRM history, researches companies and attendees, builds a Word brief per meeting and emails a 90-second summary. The session also describes "Auto" routing, which picks the mode, lead model and effort level per task; the slides cite 2.5-5x higher cost per request for the strongest model versus a right-sized one, and 10-20 points more failures on complex requests with a lighter model.

Microsoft's internal usage data (November 2026) is cited as nearly 80% of the Fortune 500 using Cowork and over 5x average growth across the top 10 industries since general availability. Customer examples include a global bank saving 56 hours across 30 tasks and a financial services firm cutting survey-to-slides from 120 to 10 minutes. These are vendor-reported figures.
