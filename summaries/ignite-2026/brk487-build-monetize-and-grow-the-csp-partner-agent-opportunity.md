# Build, Monetize, and Grow: The CSP Partner Agent Opportunity

**Speaker(s):** Naila Byrne, Jayden Lee

## Summary

The session covers the CSP partner opportunity for building and monetizing agents. It cites a Forrester TEI analysis (composite SMB customer) where partners capture roughly $23K of about $95K total three-year revenue opportunity per SMB customer, with the untapped part in modernization, data platform and AI readiness, and AI and agentic services. Partner examples (DEMV, Bespoke, CallRevu, Qualiserve) show results with Foundry, Copilot Studio and Azure OpenAI.

Why build on Microsoft: built-in distribution through 450M+ Microsoft 365 seats and Teams, one platform (build in GitHub and Copilot Studio, run and optimize in Foundry, govern with Agent 365, ground with Fabric and Microsoft IQ), and starting from the outcome by audience (Copilot Code, Copilot Studio, GitHub plus Foundry). RSM is a partner spotlight.
