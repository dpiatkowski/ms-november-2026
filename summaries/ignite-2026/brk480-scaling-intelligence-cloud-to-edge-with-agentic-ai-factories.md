# Scaling intelligence cloud to edge with agentic AI factories

**Speaker(s):** Dion Harris, Tin Stribor Sohn

## Summary

NVIDIA and Porsche join Microsoft to discuss scaling intelligence from cloud to edge with agentic AI factories. NVIDIA presents its full-stack AI factory platform (Vera Rubin NVL72, CUDA-X libraries, open models such as Nemotron, Cosmos, GR00T and Alpamayo) and agents as a new computing platform, with Azure as the place where it runs alongside Foundry, AKS and other Microsoft services.

Porsche's autonomous driving lead argues physical AI is limited by data, not models: rare long-tail scenarios and regressions in each release require a data operations shortcut that makes data reusable instead of requiring new test drives. The shown VW Group data flywheel uses Cosmos models, Azure compute, Kubernetes, Blob storage and Foundry, with 3.2 TB per hour of sensor data per vehicle (customer-reported).
