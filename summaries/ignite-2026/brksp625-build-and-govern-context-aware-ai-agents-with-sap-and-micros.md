# Build and govern context-aware AI agents with SAP and Microsoft

**Speaker(s):** Stuart Giles, Pragnya Paramita

## Summary

SAP and Microsoft explain how to build and govern context-aware agents. The argument is that building agents is becoming easy while trusting them to understand business context is hard (the slides cite 74% of organizations stuck in AI pilot mode and data quality as the leading barrier); a generic agent can approve an invoice and miss an $8,000 rebate credit that a context-aware agent with SAP's knowledge graph would catch.

SAP highlights process and industry knowledge, a suite-wide semantic model, the SAP knowledge graph and enterprise governance with certified controls (ISO 42001, 27001, 9001). The combined experience connects SAP Business AI Platform (Joule, Joule Studio, Business Data Cloud) with Microsoft IQ, Copilot Studio, Foundry and Agent2Agent connections. Figures are vendor-reported.
