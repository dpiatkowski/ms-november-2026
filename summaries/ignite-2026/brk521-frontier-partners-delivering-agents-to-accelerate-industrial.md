# Frontier Partners: Delivering agents to accelerate Industrial AI

**Speaker(s):** Lindsay Berg, Jason Langlais, Michael MacKenzie, Jonathan Sobel

## Summary

The session covers how Frontier partners deliver agents that accelerate industrial AI. It argues customers have moved from prioritizing use cases to deploying large-scale AI transformation (copilots, agentic AI, physical AI, data platform, OT security), and that partners are central (the slides cite McKinsey and Omdia on partner-led co-creation and strategic partner involvement in manufacturing projects).

The FY27 go-to-market is three steps to Frontier value: lead with the customer's ambition, design the shortest path to value around one relevant use case, then realize and expand value (measured by Azure consumed revenue, billed revenue and seats). Industry priority pillars are digital engineering, intelligent factory, resilient supply chain and customer experience, with use case, solution and proof framed as one value story. Sight Machine's CEO joins a customer and partner panel.
