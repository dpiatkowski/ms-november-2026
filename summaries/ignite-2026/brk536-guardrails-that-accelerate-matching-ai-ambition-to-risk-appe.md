# Guardrails That Accelerate: Matching AI Ambition to Risk Appetite

**Speaker(s):** Ron Pessner

## Summary

The session matches AI ambition to risk appetite, arguing the same capabilities (speed, autonomy, connected agents) that unlock value also change risk, so safety and innovation should not be a choice. It frames four critical conversations for business, IT and security: be brilliant at the basics (authentication, patching, endpoint, email and file protection, with FastTrack help), agent discovery and governance (visibility, accountability and control through Agent 365 and Perception), securing data, identities and app permissions, and continuous threat exposure management.

A fireside chat follows with Microsoft Security leadership and the slides point to aka.ms/SecureNow for engaging FastTrack.
