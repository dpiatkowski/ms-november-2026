# Frontier Media: Intelligence, Trust, and the partners who build them​

**Speaker(s):** Cara Catalane, Simon Crownshaw, Lewis Smithingham

## Summary

The session covers Frontier media: intelligence, trust and the partners who build them. It cites industry figures (PwC, IDC, IAB, WAN-IFRA, Accenture) such as up to $600B in new industry revenue by 2030 and higher returns for Frontier media firms, and frames an AI-native media company around three pillars: create smarter and earlier, grow revenue intelligently and run and deliver at scale, with a Media IQ governed intelligence layer and a trust plane.

Examples include Collective Artists Network producing film about 10x faster (3 years to 3 months), the Premier League's personalized fan experiences, the NFL Combine app and Kantar scoring creative assets in under a minute. Partners are told how to become AI-first transformation partners across build, sell and deliver, with up to $200 billion of new demand for tech services from agentic AI cited.
