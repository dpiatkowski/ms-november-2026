# Accelerating Partner Impact in EMEA for Enterprise Customers​​

**Speaker(s):** Angela Evans, Ahmed Mabrouk, Roxane Nadji Auld, Anne Sheehan

## Summary

The session covers accelerating partner impact in EMEA for enterprise customers. It sizes the EMEA FY27 addressable market at $494bn (AI business solutions $155bn, cloud and AI platform $153bn, security $11bn, per IDC and Gartner) and sets out where to grow together: sovereign cloud pipeline, depth in major accounts, ISV co-sell and Marketplace, and SI and advisory.

The FY27 enterprise go-to-market gives partners one AI-first play per solution area: be customer zero with Copilot, Cowork, Autopilot and FinOps; own the data and AI estate with Fabric and Foundry; modernize databases, apps and infrastructure to Azure; win agentic AI with Foundry and GitHub; secure AI with Microsoft 365 E7 plus Agent 365; and defend with Defender and Perception. EMEA ISV priorities and Partner of the Year winners are also covered.
