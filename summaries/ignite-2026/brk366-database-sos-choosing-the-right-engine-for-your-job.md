# Database SOS: Choosing the right engine for your job

**Speaker(s):** Vilas Sarangdhar, Jesse Sullivan, Eric Stoltze, Moeen Sunasra

## Summary

A partner panel (TCS, IBM, Tech Mahindra) and Microsoft discuss how data requirements are changing (fresh data, high-volume transactions, low-latency access for real-time AI, vector and complex search, multiple transactional and analytical sources, privacy and compliance) and how to choose the right database engine, when to combine several, and how to future-proof architecture.

Case studies include TCS moving a European bank's multi-petabyte records archive from mainframe Db2 to Azure SQL Database Hyperscale (128 TB, 192 vCores, independent storage and compute scaling, data residency controls) with lower total cost of ownership, and an IBM story on SQL Managed Instance for a multi-tenant government case-management SaaS platform. Outcomes are partner-reported.
