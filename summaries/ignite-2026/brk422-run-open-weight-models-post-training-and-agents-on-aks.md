# Run open-weight models, post-training, and agents on AKS

**Speaker(s):** Lachie Evenson, Jorge Palma, Jeremy Cohen, Shanshan Song

## Summary

The session argues Kubernetes is where AI runs (citing the CNCF survey and Gartner) and covers one AKS foundation for three workloads: serving open-weight models, retraining and post-training, and agents, which differ in compute shape, isolation and cost drivers. The loop is serve, retrain on your data, redeploy.

Announced items include AKS AI Manager to serve and retrain open-weight models (public preview), managed Kueue and KubeRay extensions (preview), TauGrid (open-source distributed training), DACS (open-source model-weight cache, preview), Project Optima for right-sized GPU VMs and Anyscale on Azure for Ray workloads (generally available). KAITO with llm-d and vLLM serves models, Ray handles post-training and MCP connects tools while AKS isolates execution. Harvey AI shares a customer view.
