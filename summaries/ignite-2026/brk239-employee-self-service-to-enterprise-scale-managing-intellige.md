# Employee Self-Service to Enterprise Scale: Managing Intelligent Agents

**Speaker(s):** Swati Gulati, Renima Malhotra, Kyle von Haden

## Summary

The session introduces the Employee Self-Service Agent in Microsoft Copilot as a central front door for employee service delivery, starting with HR and IT. It is a family of Copilot Chat agents with a Microsoft-managed core, extensible through the Copilot Chat Agent Platform and configured in Copilot Studio, with templates, connectors (SharePoint, Workday, ServiceNow, SAP SuccessFactors) and guardrails such as approved wording, attribution and permissions.

Scenarios cover knowledge retrieval (leave policy, PTO balance) and task completion (submit PTO, update an address, create or check tickets, troubleshoot VPN). A Fortune 100 US financial services company is described consolidating about 30,000 monthly HR inquiries into one agent connected to Workday and ServiceNow, with a phased rollout and evaluation set, access for 180k+ employees and success measured by answer accuracy and grounding. The session then covers plugins and managing agents at enterprise scale.
