# Build Customer-Facing Agents in Copilot Studio, Teams, and CCI

**Speaker(s):** Omar Aftab, Jason Xian, Chris Povolish

## Summary

The session covers building customer-facing agents in Copilot Studio with Contact Center Intelligence, using a harness and builder purpose-built for customer conversations. The slides cite Gartner's expectation that by 2028 at least 70% of customers will begin their journey with conversational AI.

Capabilities: multi-agent authoring (primary agent, subagents and connected agents on one canvas), omnichannel chat and voice with shared memory, voice-native settings (voice fonts, speech instructions, latency messaging, silence detection, voice biometrics), models optimized for cost and latency (fine-tuned small models; Microsoft AI Realtime speech-to-speech coming soon), mixing generative skills with deterministic topics, variables for personalization, and handoff to human agents in contact centers or Teams with context preserved. Microsoft IQ supplies grounding, and a demo is included.
