# Your On-prem estate: Act now. Reduce risk.

**Speaker(s):** John Bergin, Matt Swann

## Summary

The session argues that on-premises infrastructure is a prime target and gives a plan to minimize and secure it. Threat actors named on the slides (Linen Typhoon, Violet Typhoon, Volt Typhoon) exploit internet-facing SharePoint and edge devices, AI speeds up attacker reconnaissance and exploitation, and exploitation increasingly occurs within hours of a patch release.

Microsoft's actions include secure-by-default hardening, mitigations delivered through the Exchange Emergency Mitigation Service, and planned investments for supported SharePoint Server, Exchange Server and Skype for Business Server, such as default configuration mode and automatic patching for connected environments. Customer guidance: migrate workloads to Microsoft 365 where possible (Mail Migration Advisor, SharePoint scan, FastTrack), upgrade to Subscription Edition, keep an inventory with Health Checker/Health Analyzer, apply hardening such as Extended Protection and AMSI with Defender AV, avoid direct internet exposure via pre-authentication or reverse proxy, and apply mitigations during the patch gap.
