# Maximize your Copilot Investment: Scale with flexibility and control

**Speaker(s):** Gina Bernard, Barbara Kim, Karyl Singh

## Summary

The session explains Microsoft's commercial model for Copilot. Everyday AI (find, summarize, create) is covered by the user subscription license for best quality at a fixed price, while advanced AI for power users (Cowork, Code, Autopilot with long-running, multi-step work) is usage-based. A "plug-in hybrid car" analogy casts the license as the battery and usage billing as the gas tank, with Auto picking the model and effort so users get range without managing complexity.

Users see credit usage and limits (history by day, by experience and a cost receipt by task), and IT gets controls and Agent 365 business analytics. The slides argue that not all tasks need frontier models and commit to adding more value into the user subscription license over time. A model-release timeline and market figures come from McKinsey and are marked illustrative.
