# Scale your AI practice from AI curiosity to frontier transformation

**Speaker(s):** Angela Galecki, Dez Tsai

## Summary

TD SYNNEX describes a connected AI adoption journey for small and medium business partners, aimed at building a profitable Copilot practice beyond selling licenses. The journey covers enablement (courses and an AI-powered enablement assistant), a Discovery AI Game Plan (outcome-oriented workshops), Proof of Value (hands-on virtual proof of concept with demo flows for finance, sales, support, operations and data) and post-sale StreamOne reusable AI skills marketplace.

SMB use-case pillars include operations and admin, marketing and content, sales and support, HR and knowledge, and finance, legal and IT, with strategic focus areas such as agentic AI, security for AI and AI factory. Details are partner-reported.
