# Activate your retail intelligence: High-value agentic AI use cases

**Speaker(s):** Amy Vener, RamaRao Gannavarapu, Dan Schleef

## Summary

The session covers high-value agentic AI use cases in retail and consumer goods, arguing intelligence (turning proprietary context such as customer and loyalty data, associate knowledge and supplier relationships into action) becomes the differentiator. Use cases span assortment and categories, personalized AI shopping assistants, dynamic marketing content, demand planning, pricing and promotions, sourcing, warehousing and autonomous factory operations.

Agents need grounding in business context (Work IQ, Fabric IQ, Foundry IQ, Web IQ) and a control plane (Agent 365) for observability, security and governance. A panel includes Columbia Sportswear, and attendees are pointed to prioritizing use cases by business value, building a first three agents and the Microsoft Retail Frontier Playbook.
