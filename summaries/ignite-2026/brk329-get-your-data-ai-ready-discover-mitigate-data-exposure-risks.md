# Get your data AI-ready: Discover & mitigate data exposure risks

**Speaker(s):** Shilpa Bothra, Erin Miyake

## Summary

The session argues that AI does not create data risk but amplifies it: unknown, exposed or unprotected data can be surfaced by Copilot and agents in seconds, so data readiness is AI readiness. New AI apps and agents (cloud, local and third-party) add surfaces for sensitive data via prompts, responses, grounding and tool calls.

The recommended Purview path is see the data and understand the risks (discover and classify, with classification with precision at scale), protect the data (label content at scale, Copilot grounding based on sensitive information types, data loss prevention for Copilot and agents) and investigate incidents. Purview is described as built into the Microsoft 365 compliance boundary. The slides cite figures from the Work Trend Index, IDC and the 2026 Data Security Index.
