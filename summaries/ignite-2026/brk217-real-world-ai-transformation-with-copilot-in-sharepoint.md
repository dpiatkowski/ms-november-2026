# Real-World AI Transformation with Copilot in SharePoint​‌

**Speaker(s):** Rk Menon, Matt Wolodarsky, Amy Curley, PhD

## Summary

The session positions SharePoint as the knowledge platform behind Copilot and agents, citing it as the top work source in Copilot chat and, per Microsoft, 3 million sites and 2 billion files added daily. Copilot in SharePoint (generally available) lets people create sites, pages and lists from an idea, turn content into Office documents and interactive HTML reports, and enrich content with metadata so Copilot has better context.

Microsoft cites an average 17-percentage-point improvement in Copilot responses on content structured by Copilot in SharePoint, and across 10 co-build scenarios an average of 67.7 hours saved per scenario (a 95.7% reduction in document-workflow time). Examples include contract-obligation extraction and earnings data packs cut from 12 weeks to about 12 hours. Boehringer Ingelheim describes moving from scattered files to a "living project memory" for project tracking.

The quick-start advice is to pick a repeatable, content-heavy process, define it, ask Copilot to create a skill, test, deploy and track ROI; the product team also offers co-build proofs of concept. All figures are Microsoft-reported.
