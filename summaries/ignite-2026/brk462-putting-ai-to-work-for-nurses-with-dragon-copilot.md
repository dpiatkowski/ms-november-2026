# Putting AI to work for nurses with Dragon Copilot

**Speaker(s):** Brian Weirich, Kim Flagg Swartz

## Summary

FMOL Health describes moving from pilot to scale with Dragon Copilot for nurses. In 100 days it reports a 13.5-minute reduction in documentation latency, 1.2 million clicks saved, 37% higher mobile platform use and an 8% increase in the "nurse communication" score (customer-reported). Lessons: AI for nursing requires new habits (break the copy-forward habit, narrate care aloud, set clear charting standards, practice before go-live).

New capabilities include configurable support for any flowsheets and care settings across clinical roles (public preview, with Epic Rover recording requirements and listed limitations), a Copilot chat interface for conversation search, evidence-based guidance and internal policies, drafting nurse notes from conversations, and smart room and wearable recording. The slides note AI features are not intended to replace professional medical advice.
