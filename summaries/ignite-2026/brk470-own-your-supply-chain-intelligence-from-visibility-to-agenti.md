# Own your supply chain intelligence: From visibility to agentic action

**Speaker(s):** Dayan Rodriguez, Alex Bitiukov, Ryan Murphy

## Summary

The session covers agentic and autonomous supply chains, citing disruption costs and visibility gaps (McKinsey, Sphera) and a vision where in three to five years people set strategy and AI runs day-to-day execution, via three horizons: unified visibility with agentic workflows, hybrid operational intelligence (people, robots and AI, including physical AI in plants) and autonomous execution.

Partner solutions are C3 AI (customers include Shell, Baker Hughes, GSK) and Blue Yonder (Accel), with the slides citing outcomes such as reduced unplanned downtime, sourcing cost and inventory, and productivity gains across 19 distribution centers (reported figures). Microsoft's own Cloud Supply Chain shares how it uses AI across planning, logistics and operations to keep up with AI-driven demand.
