# Secure your business presence in the AI web with Web IQ

**Speaker(s):** Brian King, Dave Osborn

## Summary

The session argues the web is moving from the human web (help me find it) to the LLM web (help me choose) and the agentic web (do it for me), so brands need to be seen, recommended and chosen by AI. Web IQ is presented as Microsoft's real-world grounding service for agents (news, images, video, places, weather and commerce), with the slides stating it grounds most top AI platforms including Copilot, ChatGPT and Mistral.

For brands, agents rely on structured data (complete product catalogs and feeds, alt text, pricing and availability, review schema) and triangulate trust across the website, reviews, customer behavior and third-party sources. Microsoft Clarity insights flow back to show how agents see and describe a brand, and attendees learn how to claim their AI visibility.
