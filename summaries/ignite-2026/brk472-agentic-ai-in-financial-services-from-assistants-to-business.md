# Agentic AI in financial services: From assistants to business systems

**Speaker(s):** Jared Lambert, Hemanth Sundararaj, Ewan Lowe

## Summary

The session covers agentic AI in financial services, moving from assistants to business systems. It cites BCG, KPMG, McKinsey, IIF-EY and IDC data on AI's profit potential for banks and asset managers, the urgency of acting, and an IDC finding that Frontier Firms earn about 3.4x laggards' return on AI investments (all third-party estimates).

Concepts include Frontier Professionals (who use agents for multi-step workflows and rethink workflows around AI) and Frontier Firms as learning systems that capture signals from agent work and encode them into shared routines. Customer outcomes shown include productivity gains with Copilot and 200+ AI tools deployed (customer-reported), and PIMCO's EVP of Technology joins as a speaker.
