# Operate a global AKS fleet across regions, edge, and sovereign clouds​

**Speaker(s):** Schumann Ge, Simon Waight, Ameen Riaz Sherali

## Summary

The session covers operating a global AKS fleet across regions, edge and sovereign clouds. It cites Kubernetes as the infrastructure for AI and modern apps (CNCF survey and Gartner), and describes AKS everywhere (70+ Azure regions, sovereign and private cloud, multi-cloud hardware, edge).

A customer story (JetBlue) tracks a move from monolithic clusters, where pod IPs ran out of the VNet subnet and upgrades had a cluster-wide blast radius, to smaller purpose-built clusters with Azure CNI Overlay, built-in Cilium and Istio, KEDA, GitOps with Flux or Argo CD, and Microsoft Entra short-lived tokens. Azure Kubernetes Fleet Manager then provides unified operations across clusters: define configuration, policy and GitOps once, stage upgrades and expand across regions and hybrid.
