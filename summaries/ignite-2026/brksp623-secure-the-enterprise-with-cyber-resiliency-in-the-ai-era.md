# Secure the enterprise with cyber resiliency in the AI era

**Speaker(s):** Rahul Jain, Gaurav Mishra

## Summary

Lenovo presents an end-to-end cyber resiliency portfolio for the AI era: a Zero Trust portfolio spanning OS-to-cloud endpoint security, below-the-OS protection (self-healing BIOS and firmware resiliency), product and supply chain security, data protection and resiliency, detection and response, and security for AI. It sits within an AI-led digital workplace services model with Lenovo xIQ and TruScale device-as-a-service.

The framing is that AI ambition is scaling faster than enterprise readiness, citing Lenovo's Work Reborn 2026 research on governance and trust architecture gaps, and the slides describe building strength with automation, proactive monitoring and automated patching, and continuous compliance.
