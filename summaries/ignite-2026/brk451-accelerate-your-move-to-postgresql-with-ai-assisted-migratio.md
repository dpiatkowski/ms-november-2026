# Accelerate your move to PostgreSQL with AI-assisted migration

**Speaker(s):** Aditya Duvuri, Vikas Nair

## Summary

The session covers accelerating migration to PostgreSQL on Azure with AI assistance. Targets are Azure HorizonDB and Azure Database for PostgreSQL, with homogeneous migrations from PostgreSQL sources (Azure, Amazon Aurora and RDS, Google AlloyDB and Cloud SQL, on-premises) and heterogeneous migrations from Oracle. Azure DMS migrations to HorizonDB are in public preview, with offline and online (change data capture) modes and public or private connectivity.

For Oracle, the three pillars are schema and PL/SQL conversion (AI-assisted), application conversion and data migration through DMS. HorizonDB is promoted for mission-critical workloads (about 5 s failover, 99.995% SLA, under 1 ms multi-zone commits, 128 TB storage, Microsoft-reported). First Advantage shares its migration story.
