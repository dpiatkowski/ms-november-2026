# Copilot Connectors: Unlock your enterprise knowledge and workflows

**Speaker(s):** Teekam Chand Goyal, Jyoti Pal

## Summary

The session explains how Copilot Connectors bring enterprise systems into Work IQ so Copilot has context beyond Microsoft 365. It cites 350+ ready-made connectors, support for custom line-of-business connectors, permission-aware access, and Agent 365 governance, with connectors usable across Cowork, Office, Copilot Studio and custom agents.

A demo prepares an account renewal across Outlook, Teams, Confluence, Salesforce and Jira, then acts on open risks: assigning work items, updating the opportunity and drafting a contract in Word. The slides stress reusable skills, grounded and cited results, MCP Apps (interactive widgets beside the conversation), human-in-the-loop approval of changes, MCP Tasks for long-running work, and admin control to block write tools with Purview auditing. Connectors are packaged in the Microsoft Plugin Registry alongside skills and agents, and a customer deployment at EPAM is discussed.
