# Inside Microsoft: How we scale expertise with Foundry agents

**Speaker(s):** Sudhakar Marimuthu, Brett Mills-Meiner, Banani Rath

## Summary

Microsoft teams describe scaling expert review with Foundry agents. In marketing, blog review had become a backlog (2-4 weeks from draft to feedback, inconsistent standards held in product marketers' heads). The blog lead encoded a six-dimension rubric (hook, structure, clarity, substance, call to action, marketing alignment) into a Foundry-hosted grader agent with its own Entra identity, a private network and central guardrails, grounded by Work IQ and Foundry IQ. Authors now get a score in minutes, and the slides report weak posts reaching Foundry readers falling from 42% to under 1%.

The second story is Azure engineering operations, where mission-critical services for 500+ strategic customers use agents to assemble evidence from telemetry, support cases, Teams and knowledge bases to speed incident analysis and prevent repeat incidents. Figures are Microsoft-reported internal results.
