# Accelerate Growth through Partner Incentives

**Speaker(s):** John Corwin, Uriel Rootshtain

## Summary

The session covers FY27 partner investment priorities: focus on strategic workloads (Copilot, agents, data, security), reward CSP upsell, cross-sell and acquisition, differentiate incentives toward customer growth and protect ecosystem health through change-of-channel-partner policies. Microsoft-reported figures include $2.8B+ influenced incremental revenue and 20K+ engagements from Frontier Accelerate programs, and a reduction in change-of-partner events.

Copilot consumption opportunities include growth margin incentives on Microsoft 365 Business Premium and Copilot Business (E-SKU and B-SKU), Frontier Accelerate for Copilot funding for envisioning and proof of concept, Copilot Credits at no additional cost with 300+ seats of Microsoft 365 E7, a customer activation incentive and the free consumption packs offer. CDW shares a partner spotlight. Eligibility terms apply.
