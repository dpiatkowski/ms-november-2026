# Rogue agents? Not on my watch

**Speaker(s):** Craig Hyde, Jolly Mishra

## Summary

Splunk (Cisco) discusses trusted AI at scale on Azure, framing the agentic era as agents running everywhere and customers struggling with exploding machine data, siloed signals and AI multiplying the speed of poor outputs. The Splunk platform offering is organized around turning machine data into agentic action (Cisco Data Fabric), defending at machine speed with an agentic SOC and trusting AI at scale through observability for AI.

It positions a single control plane across security operations, observability and network operations, and mentions a Partner of the Year award for business transformation and Marketplace availability.
