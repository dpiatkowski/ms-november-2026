# How Microsoft got its data ready for Copilot

**Speaker(s):** Katerina Athanas, Mike Bambrick, David Johnson

## Summary

Microsoft Digital and the CISO organization describe how Microsoft got its own tenant data ready for Copilot as Customer Zero: 305K+ employees and vendors licensed for Microsoft 365 Copilot, 26.3M Copilot Chat queries a month, 694K SharePoint sites and 40 PB of data. At that scale, labeling must be automated, labels must be few and meaningful (Non-business, Public, General, Confidential, Highly Confidential), and an early committee-designed scheme confused users.

Principles include not hiding most data from AI (Copilot works within each person's permissions and shows labels), restricting only protected content through encrypted labels, DLP for Copilot and restricted content discovery, balancing security with productivity through intelligent defaults, meeting labels that govern who can record and bypass the lobby, and assessing risk by agent type. A culture of employees as first and last line of defense is stressed. Figures are Microsoft-reported.
