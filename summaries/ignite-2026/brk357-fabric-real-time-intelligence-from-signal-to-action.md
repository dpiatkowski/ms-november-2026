# Fabric Real-Time Intelligence: From signal to action

**Speaker(s):** Kim Manis, Amanda Rivera, Tony Guidici

## Summary

The session presents Fabric Real-Time Intelligence as a way to move from signal to action: streaming events, OneLake and real-time connectors on Fabric, with Fabric IQ as the context layer for agents. The slides cite platform scale (billions of real-time queries per day) and a GigaOm-benchmarked comparison versus a Confluent plus Snowflake solution on AWS for ingestion and alerting at 100 GB per hour, with the caveat that results may vary.

TransGrid Energy, which builds and operates energy and data center infrastructure, explains how Fabric IQ (ontology) and Real-Time Intelligence replaced custom ingestion pipelines, its own knowledge graph and Spark jobs, helping a small team scale while meeting security and reliability needs and mapping interconnected equipment dependencies.
