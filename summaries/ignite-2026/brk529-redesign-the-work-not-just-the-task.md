# Redesign the work, not just the task​

**Speaker(s):** Jeevan Pathuri

## Summary

A Microsoft Azure hardware systems executive argues for redesigning the work, not just the task: task-level AI gains do not automatically create better end-to-end decisions. The example is Microsoft Cloud Supply Chain's delivery readiness workflow, where answering "will every dependency be ready before a customer milestone?" involved 15+ tools and 100+ human touches, and an AI-native workflow reportedly cut root-cause tracing of one readiness blocker from about 1.5 hours to under 5 minutes (Microsoft-reported).

Three ideas apply: human ambition (people move from chasing status to owning decisions and exceptions), intelligence (connected context and measures) and trust (human checkpoints and an improvement loop). The operating model has AI recommending and executing repeatable actions within policy while humans decide, with lessons on leading the change.
