# Frontier tuning: Optimize AI agents for real-world business outcomes

**Speaker(s):** Kalyan Ayloo, Ranveer Chandra, malik Pradhan, Ben Ambrosino

## Summary

Frontier Tuning is presented as a way to build an enterprise learning loop: customers tune harnesses and models on their own data and real workflows to get better, cheaper results. The session argues that frontier models improve through reinforcement-learning environments (simulated workplaces where a model is rewarded on outcomes) and that off-the-shelf models show "jagged" performance outside their training environments.

The four steps are: encode the work into a private tuning environment (from SME interviews in Cowork, Work IQ and Fabric IQ data, prompt and tool-call trajectories, and Teams recordings); find gaps with private evals scored against SME-validated rubrics; tune the harness (skills, rubrics, prompts); then tune models for quality and later cost. An employee-onboarding example shows the anatomy of a tuning environment: tasks, judging criteria, tools and knowledge, and sample prompts, all inside the Microsoft 365 compliance boundary.
