# Agentic automation: How Copilot Studio blends workflows and agents

**Speaker(s):** Joe Fernandez

## Summary

The session shows how Copilot Studio blends deterministic workflows with agents. Workflows supply predictability and auditability; agents supply judgment and orchestration. Two patterns are described: embedding an agent as a step (agent node) in a workflow, and giving an agent a workflow it can call as a tool. The new workflow experience, now generally available, offers a visual canvas with drag-and-drop nodes, AI nodes and node-level testing.

The framing cites McKinsey on automatable work hours, IDC on agent growth by 2029 and Gartner on agentic projects at risk of cancellation for cost, unclear value or weak risk controls, positioning Copilot Studio as one place for agents, workflows and apps, with a roadmap and demo.
