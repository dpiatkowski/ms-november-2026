# Every asset, every signal: How AI is transforming asset performance

**Speaker(s):** Anko Duizer, RAJESH Ramachandran

## Summary

The session describes how AI is transforming asset performance in energy and heavy industry, built around "Your IQ" (state of the business, curated knowledge, teams and workflows) and trust. Use cases include flexible grids, asset performance, sustainability tracking, market forecasting, capital planning, subsurface insights, security for critical infrastructure and frontline support.

A BCG-sourced framework (AI-First Companies Win the Future, 2026) describes value compounding from deploy and skill (10-15% productivity uplift) to transform (30-50% gains from workflow redesign) to reinvent (up to 10x with agent-powered operations). A panel with a global chief digital officer follows, with many customer outcome claims shown on the slides that are customer-reported.
