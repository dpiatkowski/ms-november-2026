# From data silos to healthcare intelligence with Microsoft Fabric

**Speaker(s):** Kevin Horlock, Evan Lyons

## Summary

Peterborough Regional Health Centre's CIO describes moving from data silos to healthcare intelligence on Microsoft Fabric, with the centre described as the first hospital in Canada to fully launch Fabric in production. The journey: vision and roadmap, a tiered multi-year strategy (data maturity first, then scale), an enterprise data platform on Fabric integrating ERP, EMR, scheduling and IoT data, and an Operations Centre agent program with full go-live in February 2026.

Trust comes before scale: data governance principles (promote health value, protect people, prioritise equity), a responsible AI policy with an algorithmic impact assessment before applicable AI goes live, privacy compliance (PHIPA, FIPPA) and Purview visibility with sensitivity labels and DLP. Fabric IQ ontology provides shared context for agents, and the centre reports moving insights from weeks to minutes (customer-reported).
