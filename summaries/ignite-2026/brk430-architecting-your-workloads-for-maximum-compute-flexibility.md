# Architecting your workloads for maximum compute flexibility

**Speaker(s):** Rajeesh Ramachandran, Rohan Sacheti, Varun Shandilya, Chris Bailey

## Summary

The session covers architecting for compute flexibility: flexing across VM SKUs, regions and time to cut cost and improve deployment success. SKU flexibility covers adaptive placement/adaptive create (preview) that deploys an alternate SKU when the original is unavailable, compute templates (preview), Azure Compute Fleet attribute-based selection, VMSS instance mix and AKS node auto-provisioning (preview). Region flexibility includes Compute Fleet multi-region deployment, and time flexibility includes Spot and Spot Plus (preview).

Predictive scaling for Standby Pools (preview) sizes warm capacity ahead of recurring demand. Slides note capacity is not guaranteed and features are subject to supported sizes, regional availability and quota. IBM shares experience as a guest speaker.
