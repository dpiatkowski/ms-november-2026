# Optimizing AI agents to meet your goals: Grounding with Web IQ

**Speaker(s):** Knut Risvik, Husein Sharaf, Ray Zhong

## Summary

The session explains Web IQ, Microsoft's enterprise-grade web grounding service for agents, built on Bing's index with retrieval (Harrier embeddings), ranking (Peregrine) and evidence extraction (Talon) models, publisher controls, citations and provenance, zero data retention and compliance options. It is reachable through REST, MCP and SDKs and underpins Copilot, Azure and Foundry customers, and AI partners.

The key feature is Web IQ Auto: instead of an agent orchestrating many search round trips (which API, how many results, what format, whether to retry), one request lets Web IQ adapt depth to the task (latency for quick facts, coverage and freshness for multimodal discovery, completeness for multi-part research) and return compact cited evidence, with fewer calls and tokens. A nebulaONE university-platform customer story shows a student research question answered with web content, photos, news and video; figures are partner- and Microsoft-reported.
