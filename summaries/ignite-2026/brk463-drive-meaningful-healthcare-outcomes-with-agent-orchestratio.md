# Drive Meaningful Healthcare Outcomes with Agent Orchestration

**Speaker(s):** Naveen Valluri, Cara Camiolo Reddy

## Summary

Intermountain Health and Microsoft describe using agent orchestration to move patient safety from retrospective learning to proactive sensing. With 300+ safety events a day and manual triage and trend analysis as bottlenecks, three use cases are defined: automated triage (inferring triage fields from event notes, the EMR and literature), low-harm event analysis (clustering to surface emerging trends and root causes) and predictive harm prevention (forecasting recurring low-harm patterns and simulating prevention options).

Agents are validated in three layers rather than one accuracy score: benchmark (replay of about 220K past events with a locked gold set), real-time checks on every answer for evidence, confidence and safety rules, and continuous learning by comparing suggestions to staff's final fields, scored field by field across safety, calibration, coverage, robustness and operations.
