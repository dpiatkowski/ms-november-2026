# Securing edge AI and operational technology (OT) workloads

**Speaker(s):** Deepak Manohar, Dona Mukherjee, Fred Reiss

## Summary

The session covers securing edge AI and operational technology workloads using Microsoft's adaptive cloud approach (Azure Arc, Azure IoT Operations, Azure Local, Foundry Local, Device Registry). It organizes security around three assets: the industrial assets, the intelligence and the data.

For assets, it covers Azure Local (connected or disconnected) with built-in platform security and compliance benchmarks, and the Azure IoT device lifecycle: provisioning with DPS and certificate management for trusted X.509 identities, per-device authentication, firmware analysis that maps CVEs and CISA KEV findings to affected devices, and Azure Device Update with jobs for controlled over-the-air remediation. IBM contributes, and related sessions BRK438 and BRK475 are referenced.
