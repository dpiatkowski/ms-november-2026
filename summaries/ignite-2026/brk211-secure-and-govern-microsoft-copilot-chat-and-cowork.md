# Secure and Govern Microsoft Copilot Chat and Cowork

**Speaker(s):** Nathalia Borges, Sophie Ke

## Summary

The session presents Microsoft's trust model for Copilot Chat and Cowork: Microsoft's privacy, sovereignty and compliance commitments, plus tools and controls that customers configure. Its central argument is that Copilot does not create data risk but amplifies it, so "data readiness equals Copilot readiness": discover and classify sensitive content, label and protect it, control which knowledge sources Copilot may use, and enforce DLP on grounding, prompts, web search and responses.

Three pillars are named: protect what AI can access and use, safeguard sensitive data during AI interactions, and govern an AI-ready content estate. Announcements include tool governance for Copilot Cowork, DLP for Copilot grounding based on sensitive information types (public preview, January 2027), enhanced built-in sensitive information types (from January 2027), and auto-labeling scale of 500,000 files per day (up from 100K) with simulation of 20 million matched items.
