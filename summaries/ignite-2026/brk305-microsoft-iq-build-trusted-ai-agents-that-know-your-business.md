# Microsoft IQ: Build trusted AI agents that know your business

**Speaker(s):** Yitzhak Kesselman, Dipti Borkar, Dorit Zilbershot, Michel Porter

## Summary

The session argues agents are easy to build but hard to trust because they do not know the business: context is fragmented, tribal and contradictory, most agents are assembled from scratch, and most pilots fail to reach production. Agents need the right context, not just more, from trusted, governed, traceable sources.

Microsoft IQ is positioned as the unified context layer for enterprise AI, spanning work context (people, collaboration, workflows), business data and operations, and institutional knowledge, reused across assistants, plugins, skills and agents. The slides cite Gartner (October 2026) and an IDC projection of 1.3 billion agents by 2028.
