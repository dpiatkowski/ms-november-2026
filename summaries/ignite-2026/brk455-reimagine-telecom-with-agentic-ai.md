# Reimagine telecom with agentic AI

**Speaker(s):** Silvia Candiani, Jamie Gaudette, Mark Austin, Ignacio Garcia Juan Garcia Juan

## Summary

The session reimagines telecom with agentic AI. It frames an AI-native telco around four pillars (AI-powered customer experiences, intelligent business operations, autonomous network and growth beyond connectivity) and a "Telco IQ" governed intelligence layer. Operators AT&T and Vodafone share perspective, and examples cited include an AI call center built in-house then sold to others and AI-assisted network operations.

Microsoft's network team shares Customer Zero: it operates the optical and WAN backbone (800,000+ km of fiber) and has moved from copilots to 100+ digital workers with persistent identity (Entra ID, mailbox, Teams, Agent 365), such as "Pudge," a NOC agent for the WAN optical queue that resolved incidents including 50 Sev2s across 597 fiber spans since May 13 with people approving traffic changes. Figures are Microsoft-reported.
