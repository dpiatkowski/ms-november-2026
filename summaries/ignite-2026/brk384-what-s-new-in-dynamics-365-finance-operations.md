# What’s new in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations​

**Speaker(s):** Dave Barlin, Pepijn Richter

## Summary

The session covers what is new in Dynamics 365 ERP (Finance and Operations), framed by McKinsey's State of AI in 2026 data (about 90% adoption but only about 6% seeing material profit; high performers redesigned processes) and the claim that ERP is where AI is driving cost reductions. The slides also note Microsoft being named a Leader in three 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrants for cloud ERP (finance, service-centric and product-centric).

The Adecco Group's CTO describes its Dynamics 365 rollout (22 countries live, 25 by January 2027, more in progress) and an approach of choosing a material end-to-end process, standardizing and simplifying before automating, piloting in one or two countries, measuring and scaling, with clear conditions for when an agent can act and when a person must be involved. The deck is limited on product announcements.
