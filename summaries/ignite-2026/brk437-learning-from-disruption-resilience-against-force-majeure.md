# Learning from disruption: Resilience against force majeure

**Speaker(s):** Sami Kubba, David Steele

## Summary

Azure's outage communications and post-incident transparency teams describe how Azure builds and communicates resilience in a world of "force majeure" events (hardware flaws, global crises, third-party failures, geopolitical conflict). Their five pillars are speed (the slide cites about 10 minutes average to notify with automation versus 60+ manually), accuracy, discoverability, parity and transparency.

The session covers Azure's service-level reliability practices (safe deployment in gated rings, fault isolation, fault injection) and why failures cascade, then a three-act outage lifecycle: before (assess with Well-Architected Framework, Advisor and Azure Chaos Studio; remediate with multi-region design, Site Recovery, Backup and zone redundancy; listen via Service Health, Resource Health and Monitor health models), during and after. Azure Chaos Studio is shown with outage scenarios such as zone down, DNS outage, Entra ID outage and cache stampede.
