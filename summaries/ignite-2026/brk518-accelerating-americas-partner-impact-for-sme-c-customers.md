# Accelerating Americas Partner Impact for SME&C Customers​​

**Speaker(s):** Rodrigo Caserta, Steve Cohen, David Suhrie

## Summary

The session covers the SME&C Americas partner opportunity ($443B TAM in FY27 across Canada, the US and Latin America), with a country and regional Partner of the Year awards recognition and key takeaways: lead joint account planning and co-sell, invest in technical and business value skills, prioritize usage and adoption and talk ROI, and use Microsoft incentives and programs.

The FY27 channel strategy is built on activate (partners as the CSP growth engine, AI centers of excellence and agent factories), focus (Copilot, agents, data and security), transform (customer zero, managed services, agentic GTM) and scale (consistent execution systems and joint accountability), defining a "Frontier Ready Partner" that runs on AI and agents before selling them.
