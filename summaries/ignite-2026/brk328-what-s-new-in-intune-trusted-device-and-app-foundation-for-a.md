# What’s New in Intune: Trusted device and app foundation for AI

**Speaker(s):** Sangeetha Visweswaran, Kevin Odom

## Summary

The session covers what is new in Intune as a trusted device and app foundation for AI, extending management to Windows Servers (client-like server scenarios) and focusing on whether admins can trust what they see: latency, freshness and completeness of device state.

Two journeys are measured against faster clocks. Day 0 (enrollment to compliant): about 15 minutes at P90 for policies and app install commands on iOS, Mac and Windows ESP, an app check starting as soon as ESP completes instead of waiting about 60 minutes, hourly custom compliance evaluation and faster compliance reporting. Day 2 (a change landing and reporting back): fast-lane policy delivery, one sync waking MDM and the Intune management extension, real-time client-driven compliance checks (drift detected and compliant in under 5 minutes versus up to 24 hours) and more frequent app inventory. The stated north star is more than 90% of changes completing within an hour. Figures are Microsoft-reported.
