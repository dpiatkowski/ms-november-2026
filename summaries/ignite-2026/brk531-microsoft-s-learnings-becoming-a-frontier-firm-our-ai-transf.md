# Microsoft’s Learnings Becoming a Frontier Firm: Our AI Transformation Playbook

**Speaker(s):** Katy George, Cory Hrncirik

## Summary

Microsoft shares learnings from becoming a "Frontier Firm" (AI-powered but human-led) in the Microsoft Frontier Playbook. Three recipes are described: persona acceleration (tailored tools for specific roles, illustrated with a sales team using copilots and agents to expand seller bandwidth), AI-powered process redesign (starting with lean thinking to remove waste before adding agents, illustrated with supply chain workflows) and AI-first possibility (designing new work from scratch, such as a 3-week "Camp AIR" immersion and a Copilot Cowork build by a small team).

Principles include making knowledge work visible, building observability, making data AI-ready and changing behavior, and zero-basing work with a spec, evals and context stack instead of layering agents onto legacy process. The slides report pilot results (for example higher close rates and revenue per head in the sales pilot), which are Microsoft internal figures.
