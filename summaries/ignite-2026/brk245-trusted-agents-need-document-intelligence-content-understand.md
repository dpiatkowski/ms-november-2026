# Trusted agents need Document Intelligence & Content Understanding

**Speaker(s):** Chu Lahlou, Krishna Doss Mohan, Aman Dhesi

## Summary

The session argues that trustworthy agents need a knowledge-extraction system, not just a model, because unstructured documents (forms, scans, tables spanning pages, stamps, handwriting, look-alike characters, locale ambiguity) are hard to read reliably. The required properties are reliability at scale, schema-guided extraction into structured fields, trace to source with page and bounding-box location, calibrated confidence scores to route low-confidence fields to review, and lower token cost through preprocessing.

Azure Document Intelligence and Content Understanding in Foundry are shown as that system, working through parse, classify and extract. Use cases include claims, loans and KYC, accounts payable, tax returns, contract intelligence and eDiscovery, with an example from the legal-AI company Legora.
