# From Conversation to Completion: How Teams and Copilot move work ahead

**Speaker(s):** Lan Ye, Daniel Vargas

## Summary

The session frames Teams as moving AI "from me to we", with collaborative agents in channels and meetings. The slides cite 2.6x growth in weekly active agents in channels and multi-user agents in H2 2026, and organize the story around a hub for teamwork, intelligent collaboration and a trust foundation.

Hub features: one rail for conversations, threads, catch-up, reminders, agent search and community. Conversation-to-action features: Copilot in search, meeting to docs, meeting to tasks, recap sharing, and a Teams Phone agent as an AI receptionist with support for bringing your own voice agents.

The trust foundation lists Bot Shield (screen bots before they join), external recording detection, synthetic speech detection, impersonation detection and meeting controls. Several items are marked "coming soon".
