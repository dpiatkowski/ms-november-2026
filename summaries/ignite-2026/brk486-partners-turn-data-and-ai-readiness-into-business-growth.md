# Partners: Turn Data and AI Readiness into Business Growth

**Speaker(s):** Ashu Joshi, Aurora Santiago-Moore, Jesse Sullivan

## Summary

The session helps partners turn data and AI readiness into growth. It names four barriers (fragmented data, missing business context, governance risk and AI economic pressure) and three responses: connect the data and AI estate, establish trust, and optimize performance, cost and value.

Ignite announcements with partner opportunities include Fabric IQ integrations (generally available) giving business context across ChatGPT, Copilot, Excel, Fabric apps and GitHub Copilot, Fabric apps in Copilot and Power BI Desktop (preview), Fabric HorizonDB (public preview), new MAI models, Foundry updates (continuous tuning of agents) and the Microsoft IQ platform with unified context, API and playbooks. Each is tied to services and managed-service revenue opportunities.
