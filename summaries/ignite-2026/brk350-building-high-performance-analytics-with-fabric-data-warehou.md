# Building high-performance analytics with Fabric Data Warehouse

**Speaker(s):** Maraki Ketema, Rakesh Krishnan

## Summary

The session presents Fabric Data Warehouse as built for the AI era, where agents generate many queries, retry and branch rather than follow a human's single query path. Described characteristics: serverless compute separated from storage, a lake-centric design on open Delta and Parquet with shortcuts and mirroring, AI-ready intelligence close to the data, high performance for fast reasoning loops and integrated security, governance and lifecycle.

A customer example from Eastman Chemical reports migrating about 1 billion rows from eight systems and cutting call preparation from 4 hours to 40 minutes (customer-reported). The slides also frame Fabric as a unified data platform feeding Fabric IQ and Microsoft 365 Copilot.
