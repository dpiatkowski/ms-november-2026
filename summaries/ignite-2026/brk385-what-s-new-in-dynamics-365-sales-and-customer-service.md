# What’s new in Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service​

**Speaker(s):** Deva Rajamohan, Karan Nigam Nigam, Fabio Catassi, Jeremy Hallett, Kris Kuty

## Summary

The session covers agentic CRM for sales and service in Dynamics 365, with CRM plugins now available in Copilot for sales (25 skills such as pipeline prioritizer, deal catch-up, meeting prep and deal rescue), service (8, such as case triage and draft customer reply) and customer insights (3), backed by 179+ generally available MCP tools. Existing Sales Home experiences (AI meeting recap and prep, recommended actions, seller coaching) are being integrated into Cowork and the "Today" experience.

Mediterranean Shipping Company's CIO describes building a global CRM foundation (growing from about 90K users, in deployment waves over 18 months) and managing agent sprawl with a layered foundation: business instruction and context, secure access to enterprise systems, and a simplified user experience, with the first two layers built once. EY also speaks. Customer figures are as reported.
