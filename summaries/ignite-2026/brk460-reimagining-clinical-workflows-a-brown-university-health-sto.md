# Reimagining clinical workflows: A Brown University Health story

**Speaker(s):** Jonathon Dreyer, Adam Landman

## Summary

The session tells the story of Brown University Health's AI work, with Microsoft framing the move from dictation and ambient documentation to clinical assistants and connected intelligence that works across systems (a chemotherapy appointment can involve 10+ systems and 5-25 staff). Microsoft's stack includes Dragon Copilot, Microsoft Copilot, Copilot Studio, Foundry, Fabric, Microsoft IQ and Agent 365, with custom and partner agents surfaced in the clinical workflow.

Brown University Health, Rhode Island's largest private employer, describes five ways it puts AI to work (democratizing Copilot Chat for all employees, Microsoft 365 Copilot for knowledge workers, AI champions building role-based clinical agents, ambient intelligence for clinicians and research) and three agents improving care delivery and research. Figures are as reported by the organization.
