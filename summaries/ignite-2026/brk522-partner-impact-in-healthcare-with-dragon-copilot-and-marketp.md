# Partner Impact in Healthcare with Dragon Copilot and Marketplace

**Speaker(s):** Feliz Montpellier, Dinesh Samtani, Venky Surya

## Summary

The session covers partner impact in healthcare with Dragon Copilot and Microsoft Marketplace. FY27 healthcare partner priorities include accelerating upgrades to Dragon Copilot, activating clinical apps and connectors to reach the EHR install base, and building AI apps and agents for clinical use cases that partners list in Marketplace.

Partners can extend Dragon Copilot two ways: workflow apps and agents (generally available) that receive patient and encounter context and return structured insight cards in the clinician workflow, and conversational apps and agents (coming soon) through Copilot Studio, MCP and A2A. Program benefits include sandbox licenses, skilling, a clinical apps specialization and incentives. GW RhythmX describes its path to publishing on Marketplace, with partner quotes (for example Canary Speech).
