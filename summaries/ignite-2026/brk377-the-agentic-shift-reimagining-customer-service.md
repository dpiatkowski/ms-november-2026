# The Agentic Shift: Reimagining Customer Service

**Speaker(s):** Alan Ross, Christian Lewis

## Summary

The session presents agentic customer service built on Dynamics 365 Customer Service and Contact Center, extended into Microsoft Copilot. What is new in Customer Service: a modernized Copilot service workspace, customer context for accounts and contacts, AI coaching for representatives, Next Best Action across case and conversation, workforce engagement management and new support for third-party contact-center platforms. The slides note Microsoft is in both the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrants for CRM Customer Engagement Center and for Contact Center as a Service.

Extensions into Microsoft 365 include the Service Agent in Microsoft Copilot (now covering Field Service, with customer, case, email and knowledge context in Copilot Chat), a Customer Service plugin in Copilot Cowork and Autopilot for multi-step delegated work and employee helpdesk troubleshooting, and a Service Agent in Microsoft 365 with its own identity and a firewall between internal and customer conversations. Getting-started links are listed on the final slide.
