# Your Roadmap to Business Central CSP Partner Growth

**Speaker(s):** Tom Paquin, Mason Whitaker

## Summary

The session lays out a roadmap for CSP partner growth with Dynamics 365 Business Central as the "agentic system of record" for small and medium businesses. It describes Business Central as a connected ERP extended with 10,000+ partner solutions and unified with the Microsoft ecosystem, with Copilot included and consumption-priced agents (such as Sales Order and Payables agents, with more prebuilt agents coming soon, plus custom agents from Agent Builder, Copilot Studio, Foundry and ISVs).

Illustrative scenarios (fictional personas) show finance prioritizing overdue receivables with cited records and operations responding to a supplier delay, with Copilot features marked preview where applicable. Volt Technologies shares how it captures the agentic opportunity.
