# Operate AI at enterprise scale with Microsoft Foundry

**Speaker(s):** Mayunk Jain, Rong Lu, Mehrnoosh Sameki

## Summary

The session covers operating AI at enterprise scale in Foundry as a team sport: compliance, business owners, platform security, AI engineers and IT each need different controls on a fleet of agents. Every agent must answer four questions: access (models, tools, data, networks), cost, safety (prompt injection, jailbreaks, unsafe actions) and policy.

Foundry governance combines built-in controls (agent identity with blueprints and on-behalf-of, access controls, FinOps quotas and budgets, guardrails, Azure Policy) with extensions: AI Gateway for central traffic and token quotas, Agent 365 for tenant-wide agent management, and Purview and Defender. A fictional bank with a customer-service agent and a meeting-prep agent shows rules for a $10,000 transfer limit, hardship handling via an LLM judge and PII leakage via a safety classifier, enforced through Foundry Guardrails powered by the agent controls service (decisions: allow, deny, transform, escalate), with early access offered.
