# Give agents and people the business context to act with Work IQ

**Speaker(s):** Julie Koesmarno, James Oleinik

## Summary

The session argues that agents lack the business context a long-tenured employee has, and that Work IQ supplies it by extending to business apps (Dynamics 365, Power Apps, Dataverse and connectors, generally available). Business understanding uses semantic indexing of tables, columns, relationships and a customizable business glossary; business skills encode cross-system processes as reusable, versioned skills (name, description, metadata, instructions, resources) that any agent can invoke, authored in the Power Apps maker portal or via the Work IQ MCP.

A pharmaceutical-company journey shows an account manager with Copilot, then a team, then automated agentic workflows across order-to-cash, procure-to-pay and similar processes. Work IQ is reachable from Copilot, Copilot Studio, GitHub Copilot and custom agents through open protocols.
