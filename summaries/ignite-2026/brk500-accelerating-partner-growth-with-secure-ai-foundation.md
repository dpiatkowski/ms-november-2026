# Accelerating Partner Growth with Secure AI Foundation

**Speaker(s):** Cindy Batzli, Marcelo Dichtchekenian, Jenny Woodbury, Stephen Leuthold

## Summary

The session covers partner growth on a secure AI foundation. It cites Q1 FY27 partner-influenced revenue for Secure AI productivity and Windows 365 (with the usual caveat that influence is not sole causation) and Deloitte data that 43% of organizations consider their infrastructure highly prepared for AI.

Three actions are named: get data AI-ready, deliver AI-ready workspaces and secure access, and protect and respond to threats in real time. A long list of Ignite product innovations (many in preview or private preview) is shown, including Microsoft Execution Containers microVM (GA) and WSL containers (preview), Windows 365 Direct Stream (GA), Windows 365 for Agents with billing via Copilot Credits in the AI Flex Plan (GA), Purview DLP for Copilot (private preview), Business Applications in Work IQ (GA) and permissions that separate reading from acting.
