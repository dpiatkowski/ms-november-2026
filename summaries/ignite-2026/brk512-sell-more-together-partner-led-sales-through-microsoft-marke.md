# Sell more together: partner-led sales through Microsoft Marketplace

**Speaker(s):** Alison Buggia, Jason Rook

## Summary

The session explains how software companies and channel partners can drive channel-led growth through Microsoft Marketplace. It cites an industry estimate that most of the marketplace TAM will be channel-led, with the slides reporting a 2.4x increase in channel-led sales and larger channel-led deals.

Models covered: multiparty private offers (expanding to Malaysia and Saudi Arabia, nearly 40 markets in under six months), resale enabled offers combined with private offers (including a two-tier model through distributors), and channel-led repeatable revenue contracted outside Marketplace. New features include attaching services to bundled software, currency enhancements, request-private-offer, custom contract lengths up to 10 years and future-dated agreements. Cisco shares a partner perspective, and next steps are to be transactable, choose a sales motion and activate partners.
