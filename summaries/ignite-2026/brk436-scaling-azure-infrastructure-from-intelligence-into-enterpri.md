# Scaling Azure infrastructure: From intelligence into enterprise impact

**Speaker(s):** Saurabh Dighe

## Summary

The session argues AI infrastructure must be fundamentally rethought: AI adoption is still early (the slides cite the Microsoft AI Diffusion Report), agentic coding consumes vastly more tokens per task than chat, and energy and capital are binding constraints (hyperscaler capex and IEA data-center electricity scenarios). Rack-scale architectures, purpose-built silicon, new scale-up networks and liquid cooling must be brought into the fleet at scale.

Microsoft's response is optimizing across silicon, systems and software (the slides cite 4x higher throughput for Copilot workloads since the start of the year) with three silicon architectures in one Azure fleet: Maia 300 (higher tokens per megawatt than leading GPUs, per Microsoft), Cobalt 200 and Boost DPU. The session also covers AI-driven cybersecurity threats and responsible scaling in communities (water, land).
