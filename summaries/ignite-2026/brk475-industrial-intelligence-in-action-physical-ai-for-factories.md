# Industrial intelligence in action: Physical AI for factories

**Speaker(s):** Michael MacKenzie, Alfonso Rodriguez, Bhaskar Das

## Summary

The session covers physical AI for factories, tracing a path from connected operations (visibility) to industrial AI (reasoning, prediction, recommendations) to physical AI (executing in the physical world and closing the loop). Factory scenarios include asset reliability (acoustic AI and telemetry predicting failures, agents creating work orders), production flow optimization with AMRs and AGVs, exception detection with AI vision and closed-loop process and yield tuning, aiming for higher OEE, quality and auditable decisions.

The solution pattern uses Azure's adaptive cloud approach to keep cloud and on-premises symmetry, with an intelligence layer from Work IQ, Fabric IQ and Foundry IQ. A customer panel includes Unilever's IT director for manufacturing, quality and safety.
