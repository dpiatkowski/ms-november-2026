# Building an AI Operating System: The Layers of Frontier Transformation

**Speaker(s):** Alina Fu, Anna-Marie Scarberry

## Summary

The session argues that lasting AI transformation needs an end-to-end "AI operating system", and compares assembling the stack as best-of-breed, selective consolidation or a strategically integrated suite, guided by vendor count, operational overhead and risk. It cites Gartner, McKinsey and IDC figures on AI spend and adoption (for example $2.59T projected AI spend in 2026).

The layers named are intelligence (Microsoft IQ), infrastructure, productivity and collaboration (Copilot), and security and observability (Agent 365). On infrastructure it describes an Azure agentic operations stack (agent networking and security, isolation, caching and state, agent grid, sandbox, context cache, confidential AI) and announces Azure Catalyst, a connected modernization experience across apps, data and infrastructure, plus Agentic Ops for faster incident resolution. Customer Zero statistics on the slides come from Microsoft internal telemetry.
