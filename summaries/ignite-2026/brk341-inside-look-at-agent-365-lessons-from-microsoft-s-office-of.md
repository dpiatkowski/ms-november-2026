# Inside Look at Agent 365: Lessons from Microsoft's Office of the CISO

**Speaker(s):** Karen Dahmen, Vivek Vinod Sharma

## Summary

Microsoft's Office of the CISO shares lessons from running Agent 365 as Customer Zero. The mission is that agents will only be adopted at scale if they can be trusted at scale; the slides cite more than one million agents in Microsoft's estate, each to be discovered, risk-scored and governed with proactive guardrails and reactive containment.

Operating principles: risk-based rather than uniform, identity as a control plane, policy plus containment, and measuring exposure reduced. The strategy has three pillars (discover the agent estate, prioritize the risks with a shared definition of risky based on autonomy, access, blast radius and vulnerabilities, and close the gaps with remediation playbooks). The session explains how internal solutions informed Agent 365 and how distinct roles (IT admin and security admin) share policy, signals, automation and audit, followed by a demo and a plan for attendees.
