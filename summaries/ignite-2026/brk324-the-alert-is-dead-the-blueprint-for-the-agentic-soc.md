# The Alert Is Dead. The Blueprint for the Agentic SOC

**Speaker(s):** Rob Lefferts, Amir Gharib, Allie Mellen

## Summary

The session argues the alert queue cannot be the unit of work for autonomous agents and presents a blueprint for the agentic SOC. It cites Microsoft Digital Defense Report 2026 data (median under 24 hours from vulnerability discovery to weaponization, 61% of exposure unpatched past 30 days, a 32-step attack chain completed by frontier AI without human help in Microsoft testing) and two agentic attacks in the wild involving trusted-app abuse and GitHub-to-Key Vault secret theft in minutes.

The blueprint rests on the new cyber stack (signals, context, agents) operating as one system across reducing exposure, protection, and detection and response. People move from processing tasks to directing outcomes: set the mission, grant authority, handle exceptions and judge results. The session also covers how to start without starting over and keep control of autonomy.
