# Geospatial intelligence for industrial resilience and sustainability

**Speaker(s):** Yves Pitsch, Nora Zhan, Luke Pritchard

## Summary

The session explains geospatial data (vectors, rasters, point clouds and location-tagged tables) and why it is hard to manage: different formats, joins by place and time rather than keys, and measurements that models must interpret before they become facts. Microsoft Planetary Computer Pro is presented as a unified platform that ingests open, commercial and private Earth data, catalogs and secures it, and exposes queries, tiles and statistics to Fabric and OneLake, Azure Databricks, Foundry and GIS tools (ArcGIS, QGIS).

The "see, understand, predict, act" framing covers conditions and change, joining Earth data to business data, forecasting (storm impact, crop yield, habitat loss, with GeoAI models such as Aurora) and putting answers into Power BI, planning systems, Copilot and agents. EY joins as a speaker, with industry examples such as energy.
