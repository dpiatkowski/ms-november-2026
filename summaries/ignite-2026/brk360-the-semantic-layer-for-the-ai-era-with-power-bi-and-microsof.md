# The semantic layer for the AI era with Power BI and Microsoft Fabric

**Speaker(s):** Eun Hee Kim, Christian Wade

## Summary

The session positions the Power BI semantic model as the semantic layer for AI, grounding BI and AI in trusted business definitions and metrics (the slides cite 35M+ regular users of semantic models). It contrasts a typical metrics layer (basic joins and SQL aggregations) with the Power BI semantic model's row- and object-level security, time intelligence, fiscal calendars, calculation groups, DAX engine and more.

A demo shows agentic semantic modeling, where agents help build and refine models, and the session ties the semantic layer to Fabric IQ and OneLake for structured, unstructured, real-time and graph data. Analyst rankings shown on the slides carry the usual Gartner and Forrester disclaimers.
