# From 2 days to 45 minutes: Bradesco’s agentic AI transformation

**Speaker(s):** Bruno Borges, Felipe Fernandes dos Reis

## Summary

Bradesco, one of Latin America's largest banks (R$2.5T in assets, 74M+ customers, 2.2B transactions a day on average), describes its AI operating model and a cloud migration where agentic AI cut work from two days to 45 minutes per the session title. The bank's AI base is BIA, a ten-year-old customer assistant, and BRIDGE, a governed multicloud, multimodel generative AI platform with agents, harness, skills, MCP tools, guardrails, FinOps, observability and human-in-the-loop controls.

BIA Tech applies GitHub Copilot and agents across the software lifecycle (impact analysis, automated tests, AI code review, incident resolution, FinOps optimization, story writing), with an orchestrator called AgentiX. The migration story covers moving legacy on-premises workloads to the cloud using Microsoft AI across discovery, wave planning, infrastructure and code. Results and figures are as reported by Bradesco.
