# What's new in Microsoft Copilot for functional roles

**Speaker(s):** Tejas Dixit, Bassel Namih, Deepak Palled

## Summary

The session shows "Copilot for [function]" as role-specific bundles of skills, agentic orchestration, industry expertise and enterprise trust, combined with plugins that gather from and post to line-of-business systems.

Finance demo: a controller reconciles bank to books in Excel with an audit trail, then reforecasts from an ERP snapshot with an interactive canvas shared with 12 teams that flags a total that no longer ties and shows who hardcoded which cell so just that formula can be restored. Trusted data sources named include D&B, FactSet, LSEG, Moody's, S&P Global and others.

Legal demo: in-house counsel responds to a supplier's 12% energy surcharge by reviewing 18 agreements in tabular review, researching precedent, setting up scheduled automations and improving a contract playbook, then applying it at renewals. Legal partners named include Harvey, iManage, Legora and Relativity. The session also covers sales, service and other functions.
