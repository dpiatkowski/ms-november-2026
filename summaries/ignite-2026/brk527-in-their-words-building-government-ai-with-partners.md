# In Their Words: Building Government AI With Partners​

**Speaker(s):** Katherine Flesh, Mathew Taylor, Dorothy Amaro, Shera Mui, Bhaskar Kumar Rudrakshala

## Summary

The session features California's approach to government AI, with the State Chief Platform Officer, Caltrans, NVIDIA and Microsoft. It frames government priorities (better services with constrained budgets, governing AI responsibly, moving from pilots to production, trusted cloud and a partner ecosystem), cites that at least 80% of governments are expected to deploy AI agents for routine decisions by 2028, and uses "one state, two levels of AI": a statewide platform and Caltrans mission outcomes for safer roads.

The Frontier transformation theme in transportation and urban infrastructure covers continuous risk management for safety, agentic services at scale and managing physical assets with operational, location and sensor data. The FY27 go-to-market framework (listen and consult, inspire and design, empower and achieve, realize value, manage and optimize) is also shown for partners.
