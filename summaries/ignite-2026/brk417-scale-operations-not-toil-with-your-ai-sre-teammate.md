# Scale operations, not toil, with your AI SRE teammate

**Speaker(s):** Nir Mashkowski, Sanchit Mehta, Babji Talluri

## Summary

The session covers Azure SRE Agent, an AI operations teammate (generally available since March 2026) for incident response, scheduled workflows and operations toil across Azure, hybrid via Azure Arc and multi-cloud via connectors. It can be used interactively in the portal, reactively on alerts (Azure Monitor, PagerDuty, ServiceNow, HTTP triggers) with mitigation after approval, or proactively on schedules.

Slides report Microsoft internal use: 3.73M incidents processed, 380,091 mitigated end to end by the agent and a median time to mitigate of 4 minutes versus 100 minutes for humans on the same incident classes (Microsoft-reported). Enterprise controls include RBAC, managed identity, on-behalf-of, audit, human approval and tenant isolation. New items include Teams integration, Slack (private preview), Entra Agent ID, agent-to-agent support, customer-managed keys, built-in support in Azure Container Apps and Grafana dashboards. InEight shares a customer view.
