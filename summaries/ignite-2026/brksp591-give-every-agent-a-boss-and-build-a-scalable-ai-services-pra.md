# Give every agent a boss and build a scalable AI services practice

**Speaker(s):** Sarita Kamppinen, Hailey Henry

## Summary

The session covers the partner services opportunity around operating agents ("every agent needs a boss"). It cites an Omdia forecast of an AI services market growing from $85bn in 2026 to $347bn by 2031 (the slide notes approval was pending) and that only a small share of partners are positioned to deliver mature AI services, plus Microsoft and IBM figures on shadow agents and incomplete agent inventories.

Ingram Micro's view is that building agents is becoming a commodity while running them (governance, managed services and adoption) is where value moves, with operational AI services growing from 30% to 46% of the market by 2031 (Omdia) and Microsoft AI managed services growing 125% a year (Forrester TEI). The session lays out how partners can build, scale and operate the "boss" layer.
