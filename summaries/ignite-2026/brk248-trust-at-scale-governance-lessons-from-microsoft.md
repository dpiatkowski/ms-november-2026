# Trust at scale: Governance lessons from Microsoft

**Speaker(s):** Sarah Bird, Natasha Crampton

## Summary

Microsoft's Responsible AI leaders describe how they govern AI at scale. Trends cited include accelerating AI diffusion, expanding agentic capabilities, greater misuse exposure, fast-moving regulation and AI finding security vulnerabilities, shifting attention from model capability to deployment and real-world impact.

Key practices: risk-proportionate governance (simple, standard, complex and frontier-model launches get different review depth); a re-engineered Responsible AI Standard separating developer and deployer duties, tailored to models, platform services and applications, with risk-based rules aligned to the Security Development Lifecycle; and a govern, measure, manage, release process, with nearly 2,500 generative AI releases and 450+ Sensitive Uses cases reviewed from July 2025 to June 2026. Announcements: agent controls (write a policy once, enforced at every step, public preview, with Foundry and Agent 365 integrations coming soon) and an evidence loop of rubric evaluator, annotations, insights (generally available) and golden datasets.
