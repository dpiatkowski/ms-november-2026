# Deploy solutions at scale with Power Platform or Copilot Managed Runtime

**Speaker(s):** Ryan Jansen, Austin Laugesen, Zohar Raz, Nicholas Balestrino

## Summary

The session covers deploying solutions at scale with Power Platform or Copilot Managed Runtime and balancing innovation against risk through a managed platform: managed governance (environment strategy, visibility), managed operations (ALM, observability, alerts), managed transformation (usage and ROI insights) and managed security (posture, threat and data protection, identity).

Novartis describes empowering citizen developers: a 10-week structured cohort with nominated builders and expert support, with the slides reporting agents up 7.5x and makers up 2.6x, and rollout of Copilot Cowork from June 2026 with persona-based credit allowances. The session also covers setting up experimentation zones ("green zone" personal environments with environment routing, connector policies, inventory and auditing via Agent 365) and operating production assets. Figures are customer-reported.
