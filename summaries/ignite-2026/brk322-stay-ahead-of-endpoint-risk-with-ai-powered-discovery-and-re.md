# Stay ahead of endpoint risk with AI-powered discovery and remediation

**Speaker(s):** Alemeshet Alemu, Ankur Goyal

## Summary

The session addresses the gap between attackers weaponizing vulnerabilities in under a day and enterprises taking 30-60 days to remediate critical ones, citing the Microsoft Digital Defense Report 2026 (for example 1.1M+ devices running ClickFix-style attacker commands Feb-May 2026 and 1 in 200 CVEs ever exploited). It argues the endpoint is where risk becomes real and security and IT need a shared path from finding to fix.

Microsoft Perception (generally available) uses agents that work as red, blue and green teams, with signals from Purview, Entra, Defender, Intune and Sentinel. Perception for IT lets admins query Intune data in natural language, and a Vulnerability Remediation Agent turns Defender findings (including from the Risk Reduction Agent) into a tenant-specific Intune plan that runs only after approval, with saved reports that rerun on fresh data. A demo goes from a security finding to a verified fix.
