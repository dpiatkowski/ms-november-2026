# The next evolution of AI FinOps: From cost to value

**Speaker(s):** Ryan Jones, AVINASH Prakhya

## Summary

The session describes AI FinOps evolving from adoption and cost control to maximizing value, which starts with visibility into the whole AI estate. Agent 365 is positioned as the observability platform (management, governance, security, business value), with plan, control and optimize stages.

Agent 365 Cost Management in the Microsoft 365 admin center adds spending policies (hard caps, alerts, model choice), resource-scoped policies for specific agents, chargeback, cost-and-value views for Microsoft and third-party agents, spend forecasting and flags for high-cost, low-value agents, and delegated access. Agent 365 Business Analytics (Frontier, public) helps leaders understand spend, measure value and forecast scenarios. The AI Flex Plan offers a 1- or 3-year commitment, monthly or upfront payment, counting toward the Azure consumption commitment across eligible Copilot, GitHub, Foundry, Security and Microsoft IQ services. Spend figures cited come from BCG and Gartner.
