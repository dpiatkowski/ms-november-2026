# Frontier Transformation: The Trillion-Dollar Partner Opportunity

**Speaker(s):** Alexandra Zagury, Heather Deggans, David Lochridge

## Summary

The session presents Frontier transformation as a roughly $1.3 trillion total addressable partner opportunity (FY27 Microsoft estimates based on IDC), with SMB, enterprise and corporate segments. It argues AI adoption is here but trust and value lag (the slides cite KPMG and Gartner figures such as 13% of CIOs reporting significant value), and that Microsoft builds the platform (Copilot, Microsoft IQ, Agent 365) while partners deliver adoption, outcomes, security, FinOps and continuous improvement.

Guidance for partners: build the practice where margin sits (advise, deploy, manage with agent ops, FinOps and SecOps), shift offerings from product-led to outcome-led, and learn from AI-native partners (prove value internally first, invest in technical depth, rebuild delivery around humans and agents, productize what repeats). Growth drivers and inhibitors are drawn from McKinsey analysis and partner CXO interviews.
